# @myfriend_adani: editorial charter

The page documents how the Adani Group grew from the 1980s to today, where that growth intersected with government decisions (especially under Narendra Modi as Gujarat CM 2001–14 and PM since 2014), and every major controversy, investigation and legal case involving the group.

## Policy
- We publish only what is publicly available and valid on the day of posting.
- Every claim carries a label: **PROVEN** (court/regulator finding), **ALLEGED** (reported or under investigation), **DISPUTED** (denied by the group), **CLEARED** (dismissed by an authority).
- When a matter is cleared, we post the allegation, the parties involved, and the clearance, with links to the orders.
- Every graphic carries "Status as of <date>". Every caption carries its sources.
- The Adani Group's and government's responses are included on every allegation post.
- No AI-generated realistic images of real people, no fabricated documents or quotes. AI-made illustrations are labelled.
- Corrections within 24 hours, publicly, as an UPDATE post.

## Research scope (claims register backlog)
1. Origins: Ahmedabad, trading, Adani Exports (1988).
2. Gujarat era: Mundra port & SEZ land allotments, pricing vs market, CAG and court observations, environmental cases.
3. After 2014: airports (2019 bidding rules), Mumbai airport, coal, power, transmission, green energy, cement, NDTV, Dharavi, defence.
4. Overseas: Carmichael (Australia), Sri Lanka, Bangladesh power deal, Kenya, Haifa port; link to official visits where documented.
5. Cases: Hindenburg (Jan 2023) & SEBI proceedings, Supreme Court expert committee, OCCRP reporting, US DOJ/SEC case (Nov 2024), DRI coal import valuation, environmental and land disputes, political funding where documented.
6. The other side: denials, clearances, infrastructure built, governments' stated rationale.

## Preferred sources
Court orders, SEBI/CAG reports, parliamentary records, US DOJ/SEC filings, company filings and stock-exchange disclosures, Reuters, FT, The Hindu, Indian Express, Scroll, The Wire, Newslaundry, OCCRP.
